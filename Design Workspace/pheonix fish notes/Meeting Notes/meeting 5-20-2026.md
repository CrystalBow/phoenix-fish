### Tech World
- Hex grid system
	- Guns have their own attack stat
	- Melee: Attack + Weapon Attack
	- Magic: Spells (Magic + Equipment Magic)
	- Nimbleness will stay low (1-3)
- Combat Starting
	- Formation
	- Consolidation (1 space move at the start of combat)
	- Player then enemy (Enemy can go first in scripted encounters)
- Adrenaline
	- Doge Roll
	- Stronger Skills

### Magic World
- Zones
	- Can save up to 2 actions.
	- Moving across a zone costs an action
	- Adrenaline can be used to do an action immediately
	- Auto Attacking speed determined by nimbleness. (guns need to be reloaded)
	- Weapon Swap is not an action.
- Melee Attacks only can hit people in the same zone
- Ranged attacks can hit across zones
- Bosses will have cross points where melee attacks are effective
### Story
- Cooperate Gods: Architects
	- Make money and rule the world
	- Technology
	- Threat: Magic, so they split the 2 planes that sit on top of each other (ages ago), force the animal people into ignorance, and turning normal people into engineers and such.
- Their new plan (the final stage): the great school (Artfishel Intelligence)
	- The School chip: A device that grants you access to the Artfishel Intelligence from anywhere, but this interface also supervises you and can be used to control you (that last part is hush, hush). They are trying to spread it mostly on the Animal people, and some of their more loyal tech people.
#### The Tale
- Friend wants to see animal people and built a forbidden device to go over.
- Sees how Artfishel Intelligence and other tech is being used.
- Then we head out and attempt to save the animal people.
- Get in a few scuffles with the government try to save people from the great school.
	- Convince animal people that the great school is bad.
	- Shut down distribution centers.
- The architects get mad and start forcefully installing the great school into animal people and tech people a like.
	- Our heroes go to the tech world to destroy the access points
	- They also try to reveal the truth to the masses.
- Unfortunately, most of the tech world is actually fine with this or don't believe that the other world exists.
	- Our heroes are on the run and eventually escape into criminal under belly of the tech world which has portals to the magic world.
	- Keeping low profile our heroes travel the criminal under belly of the magical and tech worlds to prevent the great school from spreading any further.
- They learn more about the world and find a way to head back up and fight the Architects.
